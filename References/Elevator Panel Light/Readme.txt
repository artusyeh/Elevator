Elevator panel illumination

A small shielded, ceiling-clear lamp is mounted above the existing control-panel display. It has an aged steel backplate, hood, end caps, screws and recessed diffuser. A short-range 4900 K spotlight washes the display surface. Existing red digit materials, segment geometry, buttons, floor-update logic and main cabin lights were preserved.

Edit the fixture under Elevator Cabin / Control Panel / Display Lamp. Panel Wash controls intensity (0.08), range (1.05 m) and cone (100 degrees). Its 256-pixel soft shadow occupies one additional atlas tile. The lamp and diffuser follow the existing elevator power circuit. Emergency lighting remains independent.

Saved in Apartment and the Elevator Cabin, Elevator Landing and Entrance Interior source prefabs. The Alley entrance preview receives the update. No external or paid assets were required.
