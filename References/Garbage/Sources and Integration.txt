These support transparent/semi-transparent bags and shared collection points with protective nets. No reference photographs were reused as textures.

Main point: existing service lane, four modest bags beneath blue lightweight mesh; recycling crate, weighted hem and wall notice. Existing drainage channel remains clear.
Secondary point: two small bags beneath green mesh on a small curbside concrete collection pad, with a wall notice. No large dumpster.

Both collections and nets are reusable prefabs. Bags have separate plastic shells and editable contents; the Blender source files are in References/Garbage. Nets are actual thin cord geometry and are conformed to the bag mesh surfaces with clearance, not flat transparent sheets. Existing street, building footprints, vending machines and gameplay remain unchanged by this pass.
Only the collection point's small local light was updated; global lighting/post-processing unchanged. Final screenshots and validation are saved alongside this file.

The local collection lamp uses the original Residential Light mesh from the preceding fixture pass, with restrained neutral 4100 K light. The old nearby glowing block was disabled. Global exposure and bloom were not modified.
