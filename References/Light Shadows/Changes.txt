Corridor fixture shadow refinement

Removed the oversized, sharp fixture silhouettes on the ceiling by disabling shadow casting on the small housing, socket and bracket meshes of all 18 lobby/corridor fittings. They retain their geometry, materials and shadow receiving. The existing fluorescent tubes already had shadow casting disabled.

Retained the original downward spotlights, light intensities and layout. Adjusted each original Tube Spill point light to sit 13 cm below the fixture origin instead of 6 cm, spreading its ceiling wash and reducing the nearby metal hotspot. Spill intensity is 0.65 and range 4 m; real soft shadows from walls, floors and other room geometry are retained, with near plane/bias tuned for the placement. This is a realtime approximation of an extended fluorescent source in the existing URP pipeline. No pipeline, postprocessing or exposure changes.

Updated Apartment, Residential Corridor, Entrance Interior and Corridor Light prefabs. The old corridor fixture repair now reapplies these settings so rebuilding its housing does not restore the harsh shadow casters. No additional realtime lights remain, and the existing light/circuit references are preserved.
