Apartment security gate mounting repair

Repaired all 30 existing residential entrances on floors 2-6, plus all six entrances in the Residential Corridor source prefab. Door assemblies are positioned 10 cm farther back toward the actual wall. Gates are narrowed to match the door surround, and their depth leaves the existing timber door handle clear. Solid jamb/header returns connect the gate posts to the existing doorframe. New guide shoes connect the folding bars into the upper header and lower channel, with a continuous threshold and frame fixings. Doorbells are aligned against the wall. Existing wood leaves, room numbers, festival decoration, gate lattice geometry and locks are preserved.

The scene is saved in Edit Mode with the existing Normal starting preset. Lighting presets, household props, infrastructure and player movement were preserved.

Validation: 30/30 mounted-jamb contact rays; 30/30 complete upper/lower guide sets; 30/30 actual player approaches unobstructed. Screenshots inspected from both corridor sides and the sixth floor. Console has no errors or warnings after validation.
