Lobby: compact passage about 3.5–4 m wide, 2.7–2.9 m ceiling; green glazed square tiles to about 1.2 m, stained ochre-white plaster above. Reception glazing on one side, shallow metal mailbox bank opposite. Layered gate, glass doors and mat at street end. Receding ceiling pipes and fluorescent tubes establish depth. Eye height 1.6–1.7 m. Terrazzo aggregate has fine physical scale; reflections are broken by wear, not mirror polish.
Office: cramped working room around 3–4 m wide, desk close to reception window. Four CRT screens in two rows; telephone and logbook on the working surface, key cabinet and analog clock behind. Warm task lighting on papers contrasts with cooler ceiling light. Paper stacks, cables and personal items form clusters with usable desk space. Keep each major item editable.
Elevator landing: green two-leaf center-opening doors framed in brushed steel, red floor display above; narrow stair opening beside it. Certificates and service boxes at reachable height. Pipes follow ceiling edges and drop to equipment; damp staining follows those routes. Avoid empty wide walls.
Cabin: 1.5 by 1.6 m clear floor; pale-green steel panels, dark green worn floor, rear mirror and chrome rails at waist height. Buttons and red display in narrow side panel. Ceiling fluorescent fixture, fan and grille; material changes rather than exaggerated geometry provide detail.
Corridor: about 2.0–2.4 m clear width and low ceiling. Repeated door bays provide rhythm with varied gates, numbered plates, meters and shoe/umbrella clusters. Maintain clear center path. Warm light is sparse and behind individual closed doors. Strong single-point perspective with alternating bright/dark intervals, not uniformly bright.
Stairs: two reversing flights with intermediate landing. 160–170 mm rise, about 260 mm going, 1.2 m clear flight width. Green pipe rail about 0.9–1 m above stair pitch. Exposed concrete, peeling two-tone walls, barred landing windows and localized fluorescent light. Floor numbers are functional building identifiers, not hierarchy sorting codes.

Implementation: ground floor lobby 4 m wide by 9.2 m long, office to west, elevator landing beyond; aligned elevator shaft on east and U-stair to west. Floor-to-floor height 2.9 m, six floors. Initial visual focus is ground floor and management room. Residential unit interiors excluded. Roof and basement may extend the same stair/shaft footprints later.
