Apartment environment — initial implementation

Scene: Assets/Scenes/Apartment.unity. Opened and left in Edit Mode.
Controls: existing WASD/mouse/Shift/Esc controller retained. Look at the main entrance door within 2.7 m and press E to change scenes. E near the ground-floor elevator operates its sliding doors. Floor buttons are separate editable objects, prepared for later functionality.

Implemented: compact ground-floor lobby, reception glazing, furnished management room, mailbox bank, notices, bench, entrance court, maintenance/storage enclosure, elevator landing and accessible cabin, aligned six-floor architecture, five U-stair connections and repeating residential corridors. Unit interiors are closed. Shaft and stair footprints can extend to a basement/roof later.

Damaged plaster was evaluated, with peeling_painted_wall selected for the final visible plaster.

Comparison and remaining work:
This is an initial environment pass, below the photorealism of the supplied concept images. The layout, green lower walls, pale cracked plaster, low ceilings, utility routes, old gate/mailbox language and cool/warm lighting separation are established.
The reference office has much denser clutter, a more distinctive worn chair, black industrial CCTV equipment and richer glass scratches. Current paper, telephone, keys, fan, cabinet and some architectural fittings need further close-up detail and more selective wear. Surface textures still repeat visibly and should receive building-specific stains, chipped tile edges and paint layering. Terrazzo needs more localized damp patches and finer edge dirt; current material is not a final wet-floor treatment. The lobby and office remain more orderly than the references. Upper floors have repeating initial architecture/props, not a finished dressing pass. Shoes, shoe racks and resident-specific variations remain to be produced. Performance/occlusion profiling remains for later development.
The cabin is accessible and its doors work; elevator travel between floors, CCTV live camera feeds, functional floor buttons, residential unit interiors, basement and rooftop are not implemented. No supernatural mechanics, characters, jumpscares or story systems were added.

No paid assets, paid APIs or AI image services used. Blender MCP was unavailable; local Blender background processing was used successfully. Unity was reopened automatically to resolve a pre-existing assembly-load stall. No manual installation or restart step remains.
