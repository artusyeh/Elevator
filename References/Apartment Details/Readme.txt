Apartment ceiling services and residential details

Saved scene: Assets/Scenes/Apartment.unity
Editable groups: Ceiling Services beneath each room/stairwell; Households beneath each residential corridor. Individual entrance clusters and room service assemblies are reusable prefabs in Assets/Building/Prefabs/Common Areas.

Ceilings: 14 service assemblies cover the lobby, security room, electrical room, five residential corridors and six stair landings. Drain collectors join a continuous front-corner stack; smaller service connections join the existing residential supply/meter routes. Electrical routes feed lights, cameras, switches and alarm equipment, with junction boxes, clips and beam diversions. Added downstands, split clamps, sockets, hangers, smoke detectors, alarm boxes/bells, physical exit signs, louver grilles, localized water/paint damage and old repair patches. Water services remain away from the electrical distribution cabinet. Concealed services beyond wall/floor penetrations are implied rather than fully modelled inside inaccessible dwellings.

Residential entrances: 22 distinct clusters among 30 units; eight entrances remain sparse. Added adult/child slippers, rubber boots, open shoe racks, closed shoe cabinets, wooden stools, folded neighborhood papers, umbrella drip baskets, parcels, plants, watering cans and cleaning containers. One dwelling retains faded traditional Chinese festival paper applied directly to its door leaf. Existing doors, nameplates and intercoms were retained. Older repeated planters/cartons are disabled, preserved locally, and replaced by the household clusters.

Sources: four Poly Haven CC0 downloads: modular_pipes, modular_electric_cables, chinese_stool, rubber_boots. Original PBR albedo, normal and AO/roughness/metallic maps were retained; ARM channels packed for URP. See Sources.json for exact links and authors. Selected sockets and cast junction boxes are used from the modular kits; the complete source kits remain available. Existing free street props and PBR surfaces are reused. Slippers were modelled in Blender with shaped soles, padded toe uppers and rolled seams. Racks/cabinets, mounts, routes and detection/alarm housings are editable authored geometry. Printed paper surfaces are original artwork. No purchases or paid generation services.

Validation: 556/556 existing architectural transforms and 161/161 existing light colors/ranges/intensities unchanged; no added lights. All 22 clusters grounded and outside the 2.5 m central corridor zone; conservative symmetrical clearance 3.15 m. All 19 wall signs/alarms/grilles have physical backing. Lowest new beam/collector clearance 2.36 m. Actual player-controller tests: 18/18 full corridor walks, 30/30 apartment door approaches, 40/40 full stair ascent/descent waypoints. Sampled overhead clearance along the stair walk approximately 2.41 m. Console clear of errors and warnings after validation.

Nine screenshots were captured in Play Mode at player eye height with the existing Horror lighting preset. Both lighting presets and the L toggle are preserved. The scene is saved out of Play Mode with Normal as the original starting preset. New emergency/alarm props are environmental dressing and do not add gameplay.

Clock repair: the source clock's glass material was incorrectly opaque and covered the original textured dial and hands. It now renders as subdued transparent glass. The old floating TextMesh numbers and cylindrical hands are disabled; the original printed numerals and three properly seated source hands are visible. Updated both Apartment and the Entrance Interior source prefab. See References/Clock Repair for before/after images.
