Stair corner and railing repair — 25 November 2026

Cause
The separate landing guards did not share the flight handrails' endpoints. Their top heights differed by 40 mm, their lateral positions differed by 35 mm and their longitudinal returns differed by 140–260 mm. Raw cylinder ends also met without proper elbows. This left the loose ends, awkward crossings and disconnected corners shown in the user's screenshot.

Repair
Six landing sections and ten flight railings now form one continuous handrail route. Rounded swept pipe joins replace the separate capped cylinders at each turn. The flight rails follow the existing tread centers at 0.96 m above the steps, and meet the landing guards at the same 1.04056 m height. Intermediate turns use consistent flat U returns. The ground and top guards terminate at actual solid wall mounts; the ground mount clears the door opening.

The route is divided into six editable mesh objects at straight landing spans so it retains local lighting and useful floor groups. Neighboring ends share the same position, radius, orientation and cross-section; they have no duplicate cap faces. Fitted capsule segments follow the pipe rather than forming invisible broad barriers. New post feet are seated on the actual landings/treads. Ground-floor guard and top-floor guard coverage are retained. No stair flights, landing slabs, window treatments, lights, player movement or room layout were changed.
