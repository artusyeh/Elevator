Elevator clearance repair

Fixed the Apartment elevator's moving geometry and door pockets. The old segmented shaft back walls protruded into the cabin, the shaft side walls overlapped its outer skin, and the doorway returns and building walls occupied the door tracks. The moving floor apron also passed through the rear face of the ground-floor header wall.

The shaft now has continuous side/back walls with 60mm side clearance and 120mm rear clearance from the cabin shell, a pit and overhead clearance. Door pockets retain 25mm clearance behind their cabin-side covers. The car side panels begin behind the inner door track; widened front returns conceal the stowed panels. Landing wall pockets and their front covers have been fitted around the outer doors. Wall backs stop clear of the moving cabin front. The sill, apron, cabin floor and ceiling were resized to meet without overlapping surfaces. Door bottoms clear the raised thresholds and grooves; seals clear the moving wear strips and edges.

The shared Side Streets rear ground excludes the shaft pit footprint under the building. Exterior walking surfaces outside that footprint remain intact. The cabin and landing prefabs are updated, and the moving cabin retains dynamic flags and realtime lights. The continuous shaft and room wall recesses are in Apartment.unity. Do not rerun older cabin, landing or apartment construction scripts over this repair.
