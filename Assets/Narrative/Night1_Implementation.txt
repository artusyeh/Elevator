AUTHORING
Assets/Narrative/Resources/Narrative/Night 1.asset contains 27 ordered steps, objectives, conditions, actions, clock changes and messages. Documents are in Assets/Narrative/Documents. Silent Call.asset and Empty Elevator.asset contain editable action/wait sequences.
Scene objects under Night 1 Gameplay Hooks and Night 1 Alley Hooks contain editable bindings, trigger bounds and spatial sound sources. The 4F fixture has the flicker pattern, approach distance, buzz and blackout duration. Sound positions, volumes and sequence durations can be adjusted in Unity.
The runtime prefab owns the opening, objectives, subtitles and document/report UI. The existing Phone prefab owns editable management/message templates. Do not run the authoring setup methods after editing the content unless you intend to regenerate it.

FUNCTIONAL ENCOUNTERS — IN ORDER
1. Previous guard: optional jacket, ID, mug, note and logbook inspection. The exact requested 02:13 AM entry is the final log entry. These never gate the shift.
2. CCTV: inspection exposes the designated 3F camera fault. Turn CCTV CAMERAS off, then on in the ground-floor utility room to restore the feed.
3. 4F light: approaching the faulty fitting causes a brief blackout and creates the maintenance request.
4. Door slam: reset the local 4F lighting switch off/on and inspect the repaired fitting. A spatial slam plays behind the player; visible doors stay closed.
5. Silent call: return the Stairwell key, dock the phone, answer the ringing desk telephone; dialogue, silence, breathing and disconnect complete before the sit objective.
6. Empty elevator: sitting starts the real lift's 6F/1F trip, landing CCTV view, movement audio, bell and doors. Standing is temporarily locked; camera look remains available. Retrieve the phone afterward.
7. Alley voice: submit the report, receive leave permission, exit, cross the voice trigger and continue to the final exit for the fade and completion screen.
